Privacy Policy — Ringtone Pick Your Sound

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Developer: Dr Paul Magic
Contact: 24hourcomputers@gmail.com

Ringtone Pick Your Sound lets you record or upload a sound, trim it, and set it as your ringtone. Everything happens on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.

What the app uses, and why

What When Why
Microphone Only after you choose "Microphone" and tap Record To record a sound around you
Sound playing on your phone Only after you tap Record and accept Android's screen-capture prompt To record the audio that other apps are playing. Only audio is captured — no screen video. Phone calls and apps that block capture can't be recorded.
Audio files you pick Only when you tap "Upload your own sound" and choose a file To turn that file into a ringtone. The app only sees the file you select, and it doesn't change the original.
Contacts (optional) Only if you tap "Choose a friend" and allow access To set a ringtone for the one contact you pick, or reset it to the default. The app reads that contact's name to show a confirmation.
System settings Only when you tap "Set as ringtone" and allow "Modify system settings" To make your clip the phone's default ringtone
Notifications While recording phone audio To show that recording is on, with a Stop button

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone only. We don't collect, receive, sell, or share any of your data.
  • The app keeps one working recording in its private storage. Each new recording or upload replaces it.
  • Ringtones you save go into your phone's Ringtones folder so the phone can play them.
  • A contact's ringtone is saved in your phone's Contacts. The app doesn't keep its own copy of your contacts, names, or numbers.
  • The app remembers one setting: whether you closed the "Help and Win" banner.
  • App data is not included in Android cloud backups.

Third parties

  • None. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no ad networks, and no tracking.
  • "Help and Win" banner: the home screen shows a small banner for another free project by the same developer (Help and Win, free contest links). It's built into the app, isn't served by an ad network, and you can close it with the ✕. If you tap it, your web browser opens helpandwin.ca. The app sends no information about you. Once you're on that website, its own privacy practices apply.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can deny or revoke microphone, contacts, notification, and "Modify system settings" access at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Ringtone Pick Your Sound. The app still works without contacts.
  • Working recording: record or upload something new to replace it, or go to Settings › Apps › Ringtone Pick Your Sound › Storage › Clear storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it too.
  • Saved ringtones in the Ringtones folder stay on your phone after you uninstall the app (so your ringtone keeps working). Delete them with the Files app.
  • A contact's ringtone: tap "Reset to default" in the app, or change it in your Contacts app.

iPhone version

The iPhone version only uses the microphone. It saves the clip on your phone and shares it only when you use the share sheet. It has the same "Help and Win" banner and likewise has no accounts, analytics, or ad SDKs.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Because the app has no internet access, it can't notify you directly, so please check this page from time to time.
